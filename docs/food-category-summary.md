# Food-Category PRAL Contributions — Original Project Findings

The completed analysis evaluated 120,956 food records from 3,848 adults across two dietary recall days. Every record was mapped to one of 163 observed USDA/WWEIA categories.

The survey-weighted mean positive PRAL component was +85.44 mEq/day; the negative component was −71.26 mEq/day, resulting in a net mean of 14.17 mEq/day.

## Leading positive net food categories
| Category | Mean mEq/day |
| --- | ---: |
| Chicken, whole pieces | 3.15 |
| Eggs and omelets | 2.16 |
| Cheese | 1.65 |
| Deli and cured meat sandwiches | 1.58 |
| Egg/breakfast sandwiches | 1.53 |

## Leading negative net food categories
| Category | Mean mEq/day |
| --- | ---: |
| Coffee | −2.83 |
| Bananas | −1.19 |
| Bottled water | −1.07 |
| French fries and other fried white potatoes | −0.96 |
| Other vegetables and combinations | −0.96 |

The two-day mean PRAL quartile cut points were 2.95, 13.11, and 25.07 mEq/day. The mean was −8.20 mEq/day in the lowest quartile and 38.12 in the highest quartile.

These are **descriptive components of a calculated nutrition measure**, not statements about overall nutritional quality and not causal effects on serum bicarbonate.
